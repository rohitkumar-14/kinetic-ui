
## How It Works
The `ThreeDCardPeel` component uses advanced CSS 3D transforms (`preserve-3d`, `perspective`, `rotateX`, `rotateY`) combined with `framer-motion`'s `whileHover` spring physics. It layers two separate DIVs (a foreground and a background). When hovered, the foreground dynamically alters its `origin-top-right` rotation to peel away from the container, while injecting a glossy linear-gradient overlay to simulate the lighting change on the sticker's surface.
