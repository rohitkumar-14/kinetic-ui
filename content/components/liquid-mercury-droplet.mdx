## Architecture & Shaders

**Liquid Mercury Droplet** runs entirely on the GPU via **Three.js** and **React Three Fiber**.

1. **Vertex Displacement**: Evaluates a classic 3D Simplex noise field combined with mouse pointer screen-space distance. As the user interacts with the canvas, exponential damping propagates concentric ripples across the sphere mesh surface.
2. **Metallic Reflection Fragment**: Uses dynamic normal matrix calculations to evaluate a double Blinn-Phong specular lobe, a cubic Fresnel rim equation, and simulated horizon environment reflections with subtle chromatic aberration at grazing angles.
3. **Spring Inertia & Presentation Controls**: Supports drag and touch orbiting with automatic spring-back snapping.
