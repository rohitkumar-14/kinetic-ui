## Material Science & Optics

**Chromatic Glass Prism** harnesses physically-based transmission features in `@react-three/drei`'s `MeshTransmissionMaterial`:

- **Index of Refraction (IOR)**: Refraction describes how light bends as it enters a material. An IOR of `1.0` means no bending (like air). Typical window glass is `1.5`. Lead crystal and diamonds sit between `1.8` and `2.4`, resulting in extreme bending and internal reflections.
- **Chromatic Aberration (Dispersion)**: In optics, the refractive index varies slightly depending on the wavelength (color) of light. This causes white light to split into a rainbow spectrum when passing through non-parallel surfaces (like a prism). This effect is simulated efficiently by sampling the background texture multiple times with offset UVs for Red, Green, and Blue channels.
- **Mouse Interaction**: A hidden global event listener maps the normalized 2D cursor coordinates to a target Euler rotation array. Using `maath/easing.dampE`, the `Mesh` smooth-interpolates towards the target tilt, creating a weighty, floating interaction.
