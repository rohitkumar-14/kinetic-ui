
## How It Works
The `MagneticTooltip` leverages `framer-motion`'s `useSpring` and `useMotionValue` to track cursor position inside a bounding box. We then calculate the mathematical derivative (velocity) of the cursor's movement in real-time, and pass that value into a `useTransform` to dynamically scale, rotate, and squash the tooltip, giving it a true physical presence.
