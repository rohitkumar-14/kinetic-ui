## Anatomy

This effect relies on a fundamental SVG filter technique:
1. `feGaussianBlur`: First, we blur multiple circular `div`s so their edges overlap.
2. `feColorMatrix`: Then, we sharply increase the alpha contrast to solidify the blurred intersections into a gooey liquid bridge.

We use Framer Motion's `useSpring` with varying levels of `stiffness` and `damping` for each blob to create a natural delayed trailing effect.
