title: LighthouseAudits
description: A beautiful animated dashboard widget displaying Google Lighthouse audit scores with circular progress dials.
category: Sections
---

The \`LighthouseAudits\` component uses Framer Motion to create smooth, satisfying SVG circular progress animations for performance metrics. It's fully customizable and can dynamically adapt its accent color.
