
## How It Works

To achieve a true "infinite" pan without complex WebGL shaders, we utilize a 2x2 grid of identical cloned "tiles". 

Inside a `requestAnimationFrame` loop, we accumulate X and Y translation based on the distance of the cursor from the center of the screen. When the translation offset exceeds the width/height of a single tile, we use modulo logic to seamlessly snap it back to 0. 

Because we render a 2x2 grid, this sudden snap is completely invisible to the user, creating the illusion of infinite space.
