## Anatomy

This component relies entirely on the magic of Framer Motion's `layoutId` prop. 

By applying the same `layoutId` string to two entirely different elements in the React tree (in this case, the small thumbnail div and the large full-screen modal div), Framer Motion automatically calculates the bounding boxes of both and animates the scale, position, and border-radius smoothly between them.

The transition seamlessly handles swapping out the child contents, allowing an image to stretch into a hero banner while text fades in underneath.
