## Mechanics

This component elevates the classic GitHub contribution graph using Framer Motion for interactive physics:

1. **Spotlight Hover**: By tracking the `hoveredCell` coordinate `(r, c)`, we can calculate the Euclidean distance to every other cell. Cells within a radius of 2.5 receive a slight scale boost, creating a localized magnifying-glass effect around the cursor.
2. **Click Ripples**: On click, we log the `rippleOrigin` and the exact timestamp. We recalculate the distance from the click origin to all cells, mapping that distance to an animation `delay`. This creates a fluid shockwave traveling outward through the grid.
