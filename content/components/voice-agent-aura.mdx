## Architecture

This component is a classic example of **Vertex Displacement** combined with a **Fresnel (Rim Lighting) Shader**. 

- **GLSL Vertex Shader**: We use Stefan Gustavson's classic 3D Perlin noise implementation to displace the vertices of a high-resolution sphere (`<sphereGeometry args={[1.2, 128, 128]} />`). As the `volume` prop increases, the amplitude of the noise increases, causing the orb to warp violently.
- **GLSL Fragment Shader**: We calculate the dot product between the surface normal and the view direction to create an iridescent fresnel effect around the rim. The colors (`uColor1`, `uColor2`, `uColor3`) are interpolated based on the displacement height and the fresnel intensity.
- **React-Three-Fiber**: The `volume` uniform is continuously smoothly interpolated in a `useFrame` loop using `THREE.MathUtils.lerp` to ensure that sudden jumps in audio volume don't cause the mesh geometry to snap violently.
