## Architecture & Disassembly Mechanics

**3D Exploded View** decomposes physical industrial design into individual procedural layers:

1. **Layer Separation Kinetics**: Each sub-assembly (Sapphire glass, bezel, AMOLED screen, neural PCB, battery, chassis, and biosensor array) receives individual proportional displacement factors with smooth spring damping (`THREE.MathUtils.damp`).
2. **Spatial 3D HTML Pins**: Powered by `@react-three/drei`'s `<Html>`, annotation pins maintain spatial anchor coordinates in 3D world space while rendering high-DPI HTML callouts and responsive hover cards.
3. **Interactive Control Modes**: Can be driven externally via props (e.g. scroll progress or state) or directly manipulated through the built-in slider and orbit controls.
