## Mechanics

Creating a realistic 3D book entirely in the DOM is a masterclass in CSS `transform-style: preserve-3d` and `backface-visibility`.

1. **Hierarchy**: We establish a parent container with `perspective: 1500px` to give depth. Inside it, a container has `transform-style-3d`.
2. **Page Stacking**: Every page is absolutely positioned on top of each other. We use `z-index` manipulation to ensure the right page is always on top. When a page turns left, its `z-index` must invert relative to the other turned pages so that the most recently turned page sits on top of the left stack.
3. **Rotation**: Pages rotate around their `origin-left` on the Y-axis (`rotateY: -180`).
4. **Double-Sided Geometry**: Each page contains a Front and a Back div. The Back div starts flipped (`rotateY(180deg)`) and both have `backfaceVisibility: "hidden"`. As the parent container flips, the front vanishes and the back appears natively.
