## How It Works
The "Gooey" effect is a classic vector mathematics trick. The `SvgGooeyFilter` component renders an invisible `<svg>` block containing a complex `<filter>`. Inside this filter, it applies an `<feGaussianBlur>` to soften the edges of all child elements. Then, it immediately pipes that blurred output into an `<feColorMatrix>` that drastically increases the contrast of the alpha channel. This math forces the soft, overlapping blurred edges to become sharp again, creating the illusion of a physical liquid bridge snapping between the shapes as they get close to one another!
