## Architecture & Rendering

**Volumetric Light Beam** implements a performant approximation of atmospheric light scattering (Mie scattering):

1. **Light Cone Shader**: Instead of expensive raymarching, the light shaft is rendered as a double-sided cylinder geometry. The custom fragment shader calculates the rim-angle (Fresnel) and axial height falloff to create a soft, gradient-faded cone of light that blends additively (`THREE.AdditiveBlending`) with the scene.
2. **Dynamic Target Tracking**: A hidden dummy `Object3D` interpolates smoothly towards the 2D cursor coordinates projected into 3D space. The spotlight and volumetric cone continuously invoke `lookAt(target)` to follow the mouse effortlessly.
3. **Suspended Dust Motes**: Instanced `THREE.Points` float gently through the scene. Because the volumetric beam is rendered additively, particles appear brightly illuminated when floating inside the light shaft.
